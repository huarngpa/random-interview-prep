# Behavioral Focus

This folder is the behavioral companion to the current-loop coding and design prep.

It is intentionally focused on the two Anduril rounds that matter most here:

- Product / Customer Focus
- Behavioral Project Deep Dive

The goal is not to build a giant bank of generic STAR answers.

The goal is to prepare a small set of strong, flexible stories that can survive follow-up questions and show:

- product judgment
- real ownership
- technical depth
- calm handling of ambiguity

## How To Use This Folder

1. Pick three anchor stories that are actually different.
2. Fill in rough bullets, not polished essays.
3. Practice a `90-second`, `3-minute`, and `deep-dive` version for each.
4. Make sure each story can handle:
   - product questions
   - ownership questions
   - tradeoff questions
   - failure / learning questions

## What Strong Looks Like

- the problem was messy or important
- your role is specific
- you made a decision that mattered
- something changed because of your work
- something was hard or went wrong
- you can reflect without sounding canned

## What Weak Looks Like

- vague team-language throughout
- no clear ownership
- all context, no decision
- all architecture, no user
- nothing surprising or difficult happened
