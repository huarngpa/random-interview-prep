# 48-Hour Study Plan

This plan assumes we are optimizing for one thing:

- walk into the Anduril screen feeling that the highest-probability problems are familiar

## Block 1: Fast Coverage

Do these once, cold:

1. [Number of Islands](./problems/number-of-islands.md)
2. [Spiral Matrix](./problems/spiral-matrix.md)
3. [Rotting Oranges](./problems/rotting-oranges.md)
4. [Daily Temperatures](./problems/daily-temperatures.md)
5. [Game of Life](./problems/game-of-life.md)
6. [Group Anagrams](./problems/group-anagrams.md)

Target:

- solve each in under `20` minutes
- say the pattern and complexity out loud

## Block 2: Weakness Repair

After the first pass, sort the six into:

- automatic
- slightly shaky
- not interview ready

Then repeat the shaky ones until the pattern is immediate.

## Block 3: Pair Drills

Practice in pairs to simulate the screen:

### Pair Drill 1

- [Spiral Matrix](./problems/spiral-matrix.md)
- [Rotting Oranges](./problems/rotting-oranges.md)

### Pair Drill 2

- [Number of Islands](./problems/number-of-islands.md)
- [Daily Temperatures](./problems/daily-temperatures.md)

### Pair Drill 3

- [Group Anagrams](./problems/group-anagrams.md)
- [Game of Life](./problems/game-of-life.md)

For each pair:

- give yourself `45` minutes
- do not pause between problems
- explain as if someone is listening

## Block 4: Final Refresh

On the last day, do not cram random new problems.

Just review:

- DFS/BFS on grids
- monotonic stack invariant
- matrix boundary management
- hash-map grouping patterns

The goal is fluency, not novelty.

## Final Reminder

For this screen, you do not need to feel ready for every Anduril loop question.

You need to feel ready for:

- two clean Mediums
- with speed
- with calm
- with good narration
